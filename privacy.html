Privacy policy

Last updated 25 September 2026

In short: no adverts, no tracking cookies, no analytics, and your locations are never sold or shared with other users. Your finds and private points stay on your device, and in your own account if you sign in, where only you can read them. To work out conditions, the app sends the coordinates you choose to public data services. Those requests don’t include your name or email.

1. Who is responsible

MycoField (“we”) is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: help@mycofield.com.

We don’t collect card details (Stripe handles them), and we don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The app stores your data and sign-in session in your browser’s local storage so it works offline. These are strictly necessary for the app to work and aren’t used for tracking.

3. Services that receive data

  • Supabase stores accounts, synced data and access records, and sends sign-in emails from our own mailbox, hosted by Spacemail (Spaceship).
  • Stripe takes payments. See Stripe’s own privacy policy.
  • Vercel hosts the app.
  • Data services that receive coordinates only: when you analyse a spot or search, your browser sends the coordinates or search text to Open-Meteo (weather and elevation), OpenStreetMap (map tiles, Overpass habitat, Nominatim search), postcodes.io, Natural Resources Wales (DataMapWales), Natural England / Defra / Rural Payments Agency and NatureScot (map services hosted by Esri ArcGIS). Like any website, these services see your IP address. We don’t send them your email or account.
  • Map imagery: OpenStreetMap serves the street map, Esri (ArcGIS) the satellite imagery, OpenFreeMap the place names shown over it, and AWS Open Data the terrain tiles. Your browser fetches these directly, so like any online map they see your IP address and which areas you look at, but nothing about your account.
  • Google Fonts and a code delivery network (unpkg) serve the app’s fonts and map library.

Some of these providers process data outside the UK, including in the USA. Where they do, the transfer is covered by UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Account and synced data: until you delete your account (email us) or it has been inactive for three years.
  • Payment records: six years, as UK tax law requires.
  • Data on your device: until you clear it or uninstall the app.

5. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how we use it. The app can export all your records at any time (History → Export). Email help@mycofield.com for anything else, and we will reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

6. Children

MycoField is not aimed at children under 13, and paid accounts are for adults.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell signed-in users in the app.
