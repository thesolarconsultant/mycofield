Where the ground looks strongest right now

The highest-scoring spots in the UK on MycoField's Conditions Index, which blends the last 14 days of rain, soil moisture, night temperatures and frost with terrain and mapped habitat. Only spots where the ground type is confirmed are ranked.

Tap a pin or a card to match them up

    Before you go

    • Open in MycoField shows today’s live reading for that spot: rain, soil, cold nights, habitat and access.
    • The index is experimental and weather-led. It shows where conditions have been favourable for grassland fungi, not where anything is fruiting. Scores change daily, so check the spot in the app on the day.
    • Many pins sit on private farmland or moorland. Use public rights of way and open-access land, follow the Countryside Code or the Scottish Outdoor Access Code, and ask the landowner where you need to.
    • Look, photograph and record. Don't pick on nature reserves or Sites of Special Scientific Interest, and never eat anything because of this map.
    • Keep these pins to yourself: don't post exact locations publicly.
