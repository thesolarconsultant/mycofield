# MycoField content: legal hard rules (UK)

Checked 26 September 2026. These are hard rules for every post, caption, image, video, hashtag and
reply the content engine makes. A post that breaks one is not made. When in doubt, leave it out.
Not legal advice; the sources are listed at the end.

## 1. Advertising: CAP Code (ASA), Consumer Protection (DMCC Act 2024)
Our own social posts about MycoField are advertising, so the CAP Code applies in full.

1. **Only claims we can prove before posting** (CAP 3.7). MycoField is an experimental conditions
   guide. Never say or imply it finds, predicts or guarantees fungi, is "accurate", "proven",
   "scientific", or that the index is a probability. Allowed: "shows where recent weather and
   ground conditions have been favourable", "a guide to when and where to look".
2. **Price stated exactly**: £8 for 90 days, one payment, no subscription, no auto-renewal,
   VAT included. Never "cheap", "from £x", or a price that isn't current.
3. **"Free" only when it is truly free** (CAP 3.23): a free window names its end time, and only
   runs when it is actually switched on. The normal short preview is a "free look", never "free".
4. **No false urgency or scarcity.** "Ends at 10:45" only when true. No "only 3 left".
5. **No invented reviews, testimonials, user counts or "as seen in"** (DMCC Act 2024 bans fake
   reviews outright). Real quotes only with the person's written permission.
6. **Paid partnerships and influencer posts are labelled #ad** at the start (CAP 2.1, 2.4).
7. **No comparisons with named competitors** unless every point can be verified.

## 2. Foraging and wildlife law (England, Wales; Scotland noted)
8. **Never encourage picking for sale.** Picking wild fungi is not theft only when it is not for
   reward, sale or other commercial purpose (Theft Act 1968 s.4(3)).
9. **Never encourage picking on SSSIs or nature reserves, or of protected species.** Damaging SSSI
   features is an offence (Wildlife and Countryside Act 1981 as amended by NERC Act 2006, where
   "plants" include fungi); Schedule 8 fungi are protected; many reserves have byelaws.
   Our line is always: look, photograph, record.
10. **Never suggest going where you have no right to be.** Point to the Countryside Code (England
    and Wales) and the Scottish Outdoor Access Code; landowner permission where needed.
11. **Never publish a precise location**: no coordinates, grid references, named fields, farms,
    commons or reserves as "spots". Map visuals are regional (county-scale or wider).

## 3. Safety and health
12. **Never say or imply any fungus is edible, safe, tasty, or worth eating**, and no recipes.
    Never identify species, give identification tips or lookalike comparisons. Keep the line:
    "Never eat anything because of this app."
13. **No health, medicinal or "functional mushroom" claims** of any kind (CAP section 12).

## 4. Drugs and platform rules
14. **Never mention, hint at or wink towards psychoactive mushrooms**: psilocybin, magic mushrooms,
    "shrooms", liberty caps, Psilocybe, tripping, psychedelic art, and no coded hints
    ("you know the ones", "the little ones", 😉 next to fungi). Fresh or dried psilocybin mushrooms
    are Class A (Misuse of Drugs Act 1971, Drugs Act 2005). Meta and TikTok remove content and
    ban accounts that promote illegal drugs, and their filters already catch innocent mushroom
    brands, so our wording stays clearly about grassland fungi, waxcaps, weather and conservation.
15. **Banned hashtags**: anything drug-related (#libertycaps, #magicmushrooms, #shrooms,
    #psilocybin, #psychedelics and variants). Hashtags come only from the approved list in
    topics.json.

## 5. Credits, copyright and AI
16. **Map screenshots carry credits** in the image or caption: "© OpenStreetMap contributors";
    satellite adds "Imagery: Esri, Maxar, Earthstar Geographics"; weather figures add
    "Weather: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0)"; any NBN-derived figure adds "NBN Atlas".
17. **Only our own photos or properly licensed ones** (commercial use allowed, credit given).
    No images taken from Google, Facebook groups, iNaturalist etc. without that licence.
18. **AI-made images or video are labelled as AI** (platform AI labels via Upload-Post's
    is_ai_generated flag) and never shown as a real find, place or person.

## 6. Data and privacy
19. **No personal data in posts**: no user names, emails, records or locations.
20. **Tracking must match the privacy page.** UTM links are fine, but the site currently promises
    "no analytics". Any analytics added to read UTMs must be cookieless (or consented under PECR)
    and the privacy page updated first.

## 7. Business basics before paid promotion
21. **Weather licence**: Open-Meteo's free API is for non-commercial use only. A paid app needs their
    commercial plan.
22. **ICO data protection fee** (tier 1, £52 a year) and HMRC self-employment registration.
